01 / What it does

Three jobs, done calmly.

You are the HVAC expert. We build the front desk around the way your shop already runs.

Intake

Answers the new lead

A call you could not pick up, a late text, a web form. The front desk replies in your shop's voice and collects what a tech needs to know.

Follow-up

Checks in on quiet estimates

The replacement quote that went silent gets a polite check-in, so it is not left sitting in a pile on the desk.

Booking

Puts the visit on the board

Tune-ups and service calls are handed to your board with the address, the unit and the issue written down.

02 / Limits

What it does not do

  • It does not quote prices. Your team does that, on the job or on the phone.
  • It does not dispatch emergencies on its own. No heat and no cooling go straight to your on-call tech.
  • It does not replace 911 or the gas utility. It says so to the caller, plainly.
