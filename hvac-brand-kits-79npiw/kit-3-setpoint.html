What it does

Three jobs, done the way your office would do them.

You are the HVAC expert. We build the front desk that keeps the phone covered when your people are busy or off the clock.

01

Answers the phone

After hours and when the office is slammed, the front desk picks up in your shop's name and finds out what is wrong.

02

Takes down the job

Name, address, system type, no heat or no cooling. The details your dispatcher would ask for, in one clean note.

03

Hands it to your people

The job lands on your board. Urgent calls go to your on-call tech. Someone at your shop decides what happens next.

What it does not do
  • It does not quote prices or diagnose equipment.
  • It does not dispatch emergencies on its own. Those go to your on-call person.
  • It does not replace your dispatcher or your CSRs.
