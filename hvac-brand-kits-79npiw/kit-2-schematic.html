What it does. What it does not do.

You are the HVAC expert. We build the front desk. Here are the limits, stated before you find them.

Rated for

In scope
A.1
New leadsReplies to calls, texts and web forms in your shop's voice, and writes down what a tech needs to know.
A.2
Follow-upChecks in on the estimate that went quiet, so the replacement job is not left sitting.
A.3
The boardHands booked tune-ups and service calls to your board with the address, the unit and the issue.
A.4
After hoursPasses no heat and no cooling to your on-call tech, with the details attached.

Not rated for

Out of scope
B.1
Quoting pricesYour team does that, on the job or on the phone.
B.2
Dispatching on its ownA person on your team decides who rolls a truck.
B.3
Diagnosing equipmentIt takes the symptoms down. Your tech makes the call.
B.4
Replacing 911 or the gas utilityIt tells the caller so, plainly.
