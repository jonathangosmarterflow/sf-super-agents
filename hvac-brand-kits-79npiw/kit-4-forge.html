Straight talk

You run the shop. We build the front desk.

Here is what it does, and where it stops and hands the call to a person at your shop.

What it does

  • 01
    Answers in your shop's nameAfter hours, at lunch, and when the lines are full.
  • 02
    Takes down the jobName, address, system, no heat or no cooling, how urgent it sounds.
  • 03
    Puts it on your boardA clean note your dispatcher can act on first thing.
  • 04
    Flags the urgent onesSends them to your on-call tech, by the rules you set.

What it does not do

  • 01
    Quote pricesPricing stays with you and your techs.
  • 02
    Diagnose equipmentIt writes down symptoms. Your people do the diagnosing.
  • 03
    Dispatch emergencies on its ownEmergencies go to your on-call person. It is not a stand-in for 911 or the gas utility.
  • 04
    Replace your dispatcherIt covers the phone when your office cannot.
