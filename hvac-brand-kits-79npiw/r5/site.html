What it does

The front desk work that slips when the phones are busy.

  • Answers new leads

    New calls, web forms and texts get a prompt reply in your shop's voice, including after hours.

  • Follows up on quiet estimates

    It checks back on estimates that have gone quiet, so fewer of them get forgotten.

  • Puts the visit on your board

    When the customer is ready, the visit goes on your board for your office to confirm.

  • Hands emergencies to your on-call tech

    Urgent calls, such as no heat or no cooling, go to the tech you have on call, with the details.

What it does not do

The judgment calls stay with your team.

  • Quote prices

    It collects the details. Your team gives the price.

  • Diagnose equipment

    It notes the symptoms and the unit. Your techs make the diagnosis.

  • Dispatch on its own

    It books the visit. Who goes, and when a truck rolls, stays with your dispatcher.

  • Replace 911 or the gas utility

    For a gas smell or any danger, callers are told to call 911 or the gas utility first.
