Dark, light or both?

Both. It is normal, and with this setup it is cheap. Most modern software brands define a dark and a light version of the same brand. Here both come from one small list of colors, so the second mode is a second column of values, not a second brand.

Dark is the face of the brand: the site, the YouTube background, the slides, the icon tiles. It is what you like, and it is what makes the one blue look premium.

Light is the working mode: PDFs, anything printed, pages that sit next to white software, and a light version of the site that is ready when you want to test it.

The site ships dark, with no switch for visitors. One version to test, one number to read. The switch in this preview is only for you.

One honest risk, stated separately: a shop owner on a phone in bright sun reads a white page a little more easily than a dark one. So the first split test once there is traffic is dark against light. Until then, dark.
